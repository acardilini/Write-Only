## Round 1: first walk-through (v0.1, encounters 1–3)

The biggest finding: when the round 2 phrases don't follow from the feeling just picked, the walk reveals its agenda and stops feeling honest.

| # | Where | Note | Change | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Vegan stall | No word for anger, or for wanting to put the vegan in their place | Added "Angry" and "Want to argue back", and one-upping phrases such as "Plants feel pain too, you know". Resentment of moral rebels is established (Monin, Sawyer & Marquez 2008); the phrases are our coding | Done (v0.2) |
| 2 | All encounters | The prompts were lighter than the scene text, and it wasn't clear what you were responding to | Each prompt is now a clear question about the moment, at the same weight as the scene: "How do you feel about being asked?", "How do you feel about what Sam said?", "How do you feel, looking at it?" | Done (v0.2) |
| 3 | Smokehouse | Picking "Nothing much" led to phrases like "It's my choice what I eat", which didn't fit and made the purpose obvious | Each second-round phrase is tied to specific first-round words. When only calm words are picked, everyday thoughts follow and the walk records "prevention held" | Done (v0.2) |
| 4 | Butcher's window | Too subtle to trigger most people; we need to know whether dissonance happens at all | "Look closer" became a two-step ladder on this encounter only (her eyelashes; then her eyes, and a card: "Our own, from Hollins Farm. Six months old."). The walker decides how far to look, and that distance is recorded. Watch whether it feels repetitive. Intensity levels across participants remain an option for the pilot | Done (v0.2), to test |
| 5 | After the walk | A lot of text already, and much more with 8 encounters | Redesign as a concise, visual summary; part of the visual direction work | To do |
| 6 | The full map | Should be an actual map, like Rothgerber's figure but better, tied to the map of the town | Draw the model as the town itself, with the walker's route across it. See `map-brief.md` and the base-map sketches | In progress |
| 7 | Questions at the end | Quite a lot of questions | Fine for now; trim after the pilot | Later |
